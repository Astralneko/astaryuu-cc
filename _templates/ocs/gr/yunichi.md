# <span style="text-shadow: 2px 2px #d9213f, 2px 0px #d9213f, 2px -2px #d9213f, 0px -2px #d9213f, -2px -2px #d9213f, -2px 0px #d9213f, -2px 2px #d9213f, 0px 2px #d9213f; color: #000635">Overview</span>

Yunichi "Yun" Reverie, commonly referred to as **The Narrator**, is an entity within the logic of [Deltaverse](/fan_aus/glitchrune/deltaverse/). She was created by an entity referred to as "the Angel" in order to maintain the proper working order of the game and contain the spread of [Corruption](/arcadia/snow_phoenix/corruption/), and wields a [Parallel Daybreaker](/fan_aus/glitchrune/daybreaker/).
