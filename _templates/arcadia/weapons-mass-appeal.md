## Overview
At the edge of anyone's memory, there was a fight between the Titans of the world, ravaging the planet and returning it to the oceans. One of them, Valkyrie, ended up victorious, and stabbed her sword into the seabeds of the nascent world, forming new landmasses for the people and creatures of the world.
{% endtextbox %}{% textbox 1 %}
## Details
Like the other *Arcadian Odyssey* worlds, this world, known as [Valkyrie](/arcadia/weapons-mass-appeal/valkyrie/), is metatextually a video game. While the [corruption](/arcadia/snow-phoenix/corruption/) cascade begins in *Snow Phoenix*, [Professor Peppersen](/ocs/sp/mint/) visits this world, and brings the cascade with him, telling what he discovered to a [poltergeist](/arcadia/weapons-mass-appeal/species/poltergeist/) named [Marzipan](/ocs/wma/marzipan/).

The Titan's life force imbued the entire world with [æther](/arcadia/weapons-mass-appeal/aether/), ultimately resulting in many [sapient species](/arcadia/weapons-mass-appeal/species/). It's thought that elsewhere, other Titans exist, but none who have sailed from the coasts of what is known as Valkyrie have found such locations.
