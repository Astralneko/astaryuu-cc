Overview

Eons ago, a titanic sea dragon known as Ashtorra was put to rest by an old god, forming the Dragonspine Mountains that divide the lands of Al-Mahijo and Neverey. But these are just stories, right? Nobody would be crazy enough to try and awaken said dragon, right...?

Details

Like the other Arcadian Odyssey worlds, this world, known as Anima, is metatextually a video game. In fact, this world is where the corruption cascade begins, thanks to the actions of Professor Peppersen and Marzipan the Twilight Scythe.

Although the world of Anima has other nations, two are important for this story: Al-Mahijo and Neverey. The Slumbering Dragon, said to have created the world, is said to have come to rest underneath the Dragonspine Mountains. Therefore, Peppersen's plan to awaken the Slumbering Dragon could, if true, endanger both nations.
