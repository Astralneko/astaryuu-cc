Overview

Yunichi "Yun" Reverie, commonly referred to as The Narrator, is an entity within the logic of Deltaverse. She was created by an entity referred to as "the Angel" in order to maintain the proper working order of the game and contain the spread of Corruption, and wields a Parallel Daybreaker.
