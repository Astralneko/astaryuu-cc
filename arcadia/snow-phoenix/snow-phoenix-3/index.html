Snow Phoenix and the Slumbering Dragon is a fictitious video game created for the project Outrealm: Arcadian Odyssey. In-universe, it is the third game in the Snow Phoenix series, a series of 2D puzzle platformers created in the 2030s by developer Stitched to Life Studios. This game in particular has an in-universe release date of 2036, though the story set inside it takes place around 2040 (or 20 years in the future of when this world began development in the real world).

In-universe gameplay

In terms of how the game functions in-universe, the game is best described as "inspired by Super Paper Mario." Snow Phoenix and the Slumbering Dragon is a platformer with elements of role-playing games, chiefly puzzle-solving and action combat mechanics. However, it does not offer non-linear progression through a completely interconnected world in the way of Metroid or Castlevania, and therefore is not necessarily a Metroidvania.

The player can choose to play as either the titular Snow Phoenix or their sidekick, Patchtail Exstitcha; two players can also play as each of the two characters.

Plot

The story picks up right after Stitched Hearts 2, where, now entering the autumn, Patchtail begins working on her proof when her advisor, Professor Mint Peppersen, goes missing. Snow meets Patchtail, a lead on the case, but assumes that it's probably just an old man going crazy. Patchtail, on the other hand, insists that something must be off about it, that when she met Professor Peppersen, he seemed relatively normal. The only "off" behavior she noticed was that the professor was obsessed with a certain inky-black bottle he kept on his person at all times. Curious about what happened to her advisor, she asks to help Snow figure it out, to which Snow's boss agrees, joking that it could be considered an internship for the university student.

In the intended plot of Snow Phoenix and the Slumbering Dragon, finding Professor Peppersen would result in finding out the bottle contains the blood of the Slumbering Dragon, a being "older than dirt" said to have laid down the foundation of Anima itself. This leads to a quest to stop him from awakening the dragon whose blood he acquired, as him doing so would likely destroy all of civilization, and potentially the planet itself. However, in the instance of the game that we follow, the "blood of the Slumbering Dragon" is of a slightly different type - specifically, the inky-black substance is instead the physical manifestation of "corrupted code," harvested from the interaction of [[Machigai]] with normal assets of Snow Phoenix and the Slumbering Dragon. The professor sees this corruption as a good thing - it's what allowed him and everyone else in the game (aside from Machigai) to think outside the box, right? He even managed to enter the world of Weapons of Mass Appeal and discover [Twilight Scythe] Marzipan, surely that's an achievement of the ages?

However, the corruption has affected the landscape, which given prior Anima events, is likely a bad omen. Thus, Snow and Patchtail try to stop the Professor, much as they do in the intended timeline. Still, he forces a "wild corruption" out of Machigai in the scene where Snow and Patches are supposed to fight him, around the midpoint of the game. Unlike in the intended timeline, this action successfully awakens the titular Slumbering Dragon of the world of Snow Phoenix and the Slumbering Dragon itself. As a gag, it initially seems that the Slumbering Dragon is Aster Cometwing before being revealed to be awakening for real; in an adrenaline-fueled moment, Aster flies the three Anima-natives as far into the sky as she can, preventing them from joining the rest of their world and becoming part of the first "training key" for the "Final Angel".
