## Overview
Eons ago, a titanic sea dragon known as Ashtorra was put to rest by an old god, forming the Dragonspine Mountains that divide the lands of Al-Mahijo and Neverey. But these are just stories, right? Nobody would be crazy enough to try and awaken said dragon, right...?
{% endtextbox %}{% textbox 1 %}
## Details
Like the other *Arcadian Odyssey* worlds, this world, known as [Anima](/arcadia/snow-phoenix/anima/), is metatextually a video game. In fact, this world is where the [corruption](/arcadia/snow-phoenix/corruption/) cascade begins, thanks to the actions of [Professor Peppersen](/ocs/sp/mint/) and [Marzipan the Twilight Scythe](/ocs/wma/marzipan/).

Although the world of Anima has other nations, two are important for this story: [Al-Mahijo](/arcadia/snow-phoenix/al-mahijo/) and [Neverey](/arcadia/snow-phoenix/neverey/). The Slumbering Dragon, said to have created the world, is said to have come to rest underneath the [Dragonspine Mountains](/arcadia/snow-phoenix/dragonspine/). Therefore, Peppersen's plan to awaken the Slumbering Dragon could, if true, endanger both nations.
