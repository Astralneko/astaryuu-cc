<span style="font-family: 'AH 'Amera-Macheda'">This font was created primarily to hold Debazi-language text. It does, however, support most Latin-script text.</span>

<span style="font-family: 'AH 'Amera-Macheda'">Like AH Kardon Standard, AH 'Amera-Macheda is already pretty bold, so it can look unreadable in bold tags depending on how your font renderer generates bold fonts... I will likely make a thinner version at some point, but not right now.</span>

<span style="font-family: 'AH 'Amera-Macheda'">AH 'Amera-Macheda uses codes for the various logographs. Such codes are surrounded by the dollar sign, like $sun$. A list of codes can be found on the sections on the Debazi language.</span>
